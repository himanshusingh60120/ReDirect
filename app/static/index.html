Every 404 gets a destination. Where the words in a broken path line up exactly with a live page, that page wins outright. Where they don't, the rarest shared words decide — a company or product name outweighs a dozen ordinary words.
Wrappers are unpicked first. A login URL carrying redirect_to is matched on the page it was trying to reach, and /amp/ paths are matched on the canonical page.
Tag and topic pages are marked for review. They have no single article equivalent, so they land on the nearest hub and show up under Needs review.
